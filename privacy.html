Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-28

This policy covers the app Paced., listed on the App Store as Paced Music. The app is owned by Saad Ali Baig.

What we collect

Sign in with Apple

We store your Apple user ID and your email. The email can be Apple's private relay address. If you share your name, we store it. We do not use the name.

Our server's sign-in service also records the IP address and device type of your sessions.

Saved songs

The songs in your playlist are saved on our server. For each song we save the Apple Music ID, title, artist, tempo in BPM, source link, artwork link, how many times you played it, and the date you added it.

Purchases

When you subscribe, your account ID is attached to the subscription at Apple. Our server saves your plan, product, original transaction ID, dates, and your monthly use counters.

Usage

We count song loads and song adds. We keep a record of each, with your account ID.

Free limits

Apple's DeviceCheck stores 2 yes/no flags on your device at Apple: free first run used, and free extra search used. A DeviceCheck token is sent to our server only when you use those free actions.

Shared tempo list

When we find the tempo of a song, we save the title, artist, BPM and source link on our server. This is not linked to you.

Apple Music

The app reads your Apple Music permission, subscription status and country on your phone. Song searches go from your phone to Apple Music.

Server logs

Our server logs can contain request details such as IP address for a short time.

What we do not do

No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no crash reporting. The only third-party code in the app is Supabase's Swift library.

Why

  • Sign-in data: so you can sign in with Apple.
  • Saved songs: so your playlist comes back on every device.
  • Usage records: to enforce plan limits.
  • DeviceCheck flags: so free limits cannot be reset.
  • Song details sent to Anthropic: to find tempos.
  • Shared tempo list: so a tempo we found can be reused for other users.

Who gets data

Apple

Apple handles Sign in with Apple, your subscription, the DeviceCheck flags, and song searches from your phone to Apple Music.

Supabase

Our server data is stored with Supabase, in Tokyo, Japan.

Anthropic

To find tempos, our server sends song titles, artist names and genre names to Anthropic, an AI service. We do not send your name, email, account ID, device data or location. Anthropic deletes these inputs and outputs within 30 days. It may keep them longer if they are flagged for misuse or if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Sign-in sessions (IP address and device type): until you sign out or delete your account.
  • Saved songs, purchase records and usage records: until you delete your account.
  • DeviceCheck flags: they stay at Apple after you delete your account or reinstall the app. This is on purpose, so free limits cannot be reset.
  • Shared tempo list: no time limit. It is not deleted with your account.
  • Server logs: a short time.
  • Anthropic: deleted within 30 days, unless flagged for misuse or required by law.

Delete your data

In the app, go to Settings, then Delete account. This revokes the Apple sign-in link. It deletes your saved songs, profile, usage records and purchase records from our server. It also clears the app's data on your phone.

It does not remove:

  • The DeviceCheck flags at Apple.
  • Your App Store subscription. Cancel it in your Apple ID settings. Apple keeps its own purchase records.
  • The shared tempo list.
  • Server logs, until they expire.
  • What Anthropic keeps under its own policy.

You can also email a deletion request to saad.ali7892@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Your steps, pace and motion data stay on your phone only. So does your location. GPS is used in Outdoor mode only, including in the background in that mode. We do not store or send this data.

Songs you import from your files, and their tempos, also stay on your phone only. Deleting your account does not remove imported files. Delete them in the app, or delete the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
